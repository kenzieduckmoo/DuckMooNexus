# Warband profession board — October 23, 2026

New native page: `/professions`, with a sidebar entry and PostgreSQL-backed `/api/professions`. The board combines recorded expansion skill/recipe coverage, Planner concentration pools, newer Omni currency observations matched by exact currency ID, and observed specialization knowledge. Filters include character, profession, expansion, search, projected-at-cap, unspent knowledge, missing concentration samples and sorting. Midnight is the default expansion; large lists display progressively.

Concentration forecasts require a valid observed amount/cap, timestamp and regeneration rate. Unknown rate stays a recorded amount, future/invalid samples stay unavailable, and projections cap at the recorded maximum. Forecasts assume no unrecorded spending and are labeled separately from actual observations. Knowledge keeps its own observation age/source. Gathering/secondary professions without concentration are not presented as missing crafting pools.

Patron cards and the daily earnings chart use retained completed patron-order payments after recorded consortium cuts and before reagent costs. No inference of material profit, order availability or upcoming payouts is made. The earnings period can be 7, 30 or 90 days. Financial records are by character/profession, not expansion; multiple displayed expansion cards share one earnings pair and totals count it once. Incomplete payment fields remain unknown or explicitly partial.

Card links carry the chosen character and profession into Crafting Desk and the patron financial workspace. Financials now accepts those incoming filters. Crafting Desk and character profession panels link back to the board. Freshness follows normal source revisions and periodic refreshes; forecast clocks advance without requiring manual updates. Characters without a captured expansion sample remain identified as coverage gaps, not assumed unskilled.

Real source inspection exposed child expansion rows historically placed under the wrong open parent profession. The board identifies recognized expansion names by child skill-line ID, chooses the newest skill observation and avoids trusting a mismatched parent's recipe count. Folio 0.5.3 rejects stale current-profession transitions and filters child rows by their explicit parent. Planner 0.5.3 prefers each child's own parent name. Existing source histories, notes/settings and SavedVariables are preserved. Focused mocked context/projection checks and TypeScript checking passed; production build and live navigation checks are recorded separately below.

The independent Omni journal also completed its first real acknowledged prefix rotation with no reported overflow. Its historical PostgreSQL event records and recovered current profiles remained present. This is evidence of the rotation path, not a claim of complete gameplay coverage.

Live verification: the production build succeeded, the board loaded real recorded profession/earnings data, character/profession filters resolved the intended single card, and both patron-finance and Crafting Desk links carried the selected character/profession correctly. Captured data continued refreshing while the board was open. An installed Folio/Planner 0.5.3 preview and rollback source copies were prepared; their new context guards still need a subsequent actual client load. Screenshots and character-specific data remain local and are excluded from publication.
